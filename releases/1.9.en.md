# HakuX-Mali 1.9

Android versionCode **41**. Supported devices include ANBERNIC RG477M, RG476H, RG Vita Pro, and RG Rotate.

## Game details

- Long-press a game card or press **Y / Menu** on the focused card to open its details from the library, search, or recent games. The page includes launch, per-game settings, save-state slots, cover management, and full ISO to XISO conversion where applicable.
- **Mods & cheats** supports importing hakuX-format ZIP packs or a cheats.txt file. Imported items are enabled manually and take effect on the next launch. Stop the game before importing or removing items.
- Export this game's Xbox **UDATA and TDATA** to a ZIP in a chosen folder, or import previously saved data with a preview. Other title IDs are skipped. Emulator save states remain available in their own slot section.

## Drivers

- **Unified 0.4.64 AFBC** is selectable under **Settings → Graphics → GPU driver** on ARM64 with Android 12 or later. Its Mali-G615 changes keep eligible BGRA snapshots compressed, correct Fable color write masks, and handle empty scissors. [Unified 0.4.64 release](https://github.com/Wannamingo/Unified/releases/tag/v0.4.64)
- Updates preserve the selected driver. Unified 0.4.53, system Vulkan, and ZIP import are available in the existing menu.
- Unified 0.4.91 is a separate Vulkan 1.3 Lab prerelease. The experimental texture-size handoff was tested on RG477M with a separate test APK. [Unified releases](https://github.com/Wannamingo/Unified/releases)

## Testing

- The game details test build was used on RG477M to import and apply a mod and a cheat, then export and reimport Fable save data.
- Unified 0.4.64 was previously checked on RG477M in Fable from slot 1 and in Far Cry Evolution at 2× past the bay flyover.
- The release excludes BIOS files, game images, and personal save data.
